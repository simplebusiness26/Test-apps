# HABITAT V2 — property repairs and compliance roadmap

Status: foundation in development, 4 November 2026. The existing site remains a **synthetic-only pilot**; V2 is not deployed or ready for real resident data. Repository boundary: `apps/habitat/`. No secrets, real tenant information or production dumps in this public repository. No new repository, paid service or changes to other apps.

## Product and users
A resident reports facts and evidence; a landlord or authorised manager assesses, selects a trusted contractor and coordinates the case; the contractor accepts, arranges access, makes safe where necessary, completes work, submits an invoice and the landlord reviews closure. The resident can dispute completion or report recurrence. Mobile-first, plain English, keyboard-accessible interfaces with obvious next actions and emergency guidance. Engineering calculations stay optional and are not the main user journey.

## Architecture and security boundaries
Keep one HABITAT app, Worker API and D1. Add additive, versioned schema and explicit migrations; no implicit reset. Use a separate verified staging Worker/D1 only if the bridge and free-tier capacity are established without affecting pilot; otherwise local isolated SQLite until available. Choose verified email provider, private file storage and identity method only after checking free-tier limits, quotas, verification and account/card requirements. No false assurance that delivery or push works. Design for retries, provider outages and provider-verified delivery; do not send real messages during tests.

Core entities: organisations, memberships (owner/manager), users and verified identities, properties, resident-property memberships, contractor businesses and landlord-specific directory entries, case-scoped contractor assignments, cases and immutable original report, safety facts, priority assessments/history, appointments, job transitions, files and provenance, shared/private conversation threads and participants, messages, quotes and versions/lines/decisions, invoices/lines/payment records, legal assessments/rule versions/deadlines/evidence, notification outbox/attempts/acknowledgements and audit events. IDs opaque; timestamps UTC with explicit local display and legal calendar; amounts integer minor units and currency. Referential constraints and transactional updates must preserve history.

Every query scopes by organisation and user membership plus property or case participation; a contractor identity may belong to multiple landlords but must only access specifically granted cases. Directory membership is not case access. Server derives actor/organisation, never trusts client IDs or roles. Resident sees their authorised property/case only; contractors see shared and their own private thread only while authorised. Former contractors lose current access after removal but their historic messages remain with appropriate visibility to remaining participants; never leak another contractor's private thread. Retain actor snapshots when membership is removed. Define controls for multi-contractor history and access before implementation.

Account creation verifies email and invitation ownership; single-use, random, hashed expiring tokens bound to intended role, organisation, property/case and recipient; invalidate on acceptance/revocation, enforce replay/rate limits. Manager MFA, secure sessions, rotation/revocation, CSRF/origin protection, account recovery and anti-enumeration are release gates. Files are private, authenticated, validated by actual content and size, scanned/sanitised where appropriate, with original/derivative provenance and short-lived access; no public URLs. Document controller, processors, lawful basis, minimisation, retention, rights requests, audit access, deletion exceptions, incident response and backups/restore. Avoid sensitive data in logs. Assess DPIA and obtain professional privacy/security review before real data.

## Workflows and operational rules
- Resident chooses category, describes issue, uploads evidence and answers brief safety questions. New case starts **GREY / awaiting assessment**; a separate prominent possible-danger flag cannot be cleared by priority selection. Show specific practical emergency guidance and emergency-service advice where appropriate. The resident does not select a formal legal classification.
- Manager records RED emergency, AMBER urgent, BLUE routine or GREY awaiting assessment; changes carry actor, time, old/new values and reason; preserve all initial facts and assessments. Operational priority never determines legal deadlines or clears hazard evidence. Decisions are reversible only through new audited events.
- Landlord selects from their directory; invite only to that case. Contractor lifecycle: assigned, accepted, appointment scheduled, arrived, work started, made safe where applicable, repair completed, awaiting review, closed; cancelled/reassigned preserve prior history. Support explicit exceptions, concurrent workers and appointment updates rather than assuming one linear path. Made safe and permanent repair are separate evidence-backed facts. A disputed closure reopens review without deleting history.
- Shared case conversation includes resident, authorised managers and active invited contractor; separate per-contractor private commercial thread excludes resident and other contractors. Append-only messages, authenticated attachments, timestamps, scoped visibility and access revocation.
- Dashboard action lists: assessment, unassigned jobs, contractor response, emergencies/deadlines, appointments, completion, quotes and invoices. No unnecessary charts. Accessible colour names/icons in addition to colour.
- Scheduled outbox evaluates unacknowledged reports, assignments, unanswered invitations, missed check-ins, make-safe, overdue repairs, disputes and invoices. Configurable policies with backup contacts and mandatory emergency visibility. Idempotency, leases, retries/backoff, dead-letter/manual recovery and deduplication prevent duplicates and missed work; record queued, attempted, provider accepted, confirmed delivered (only on genuine evidence), failed and acknowledged separately. Email first; push only after permission/support and end-to-end verification. A notification is not a substitute for emergency services.
- Quote versions contain labour/materials/other lines, VAT assumptions and currency, submit/approve/reject audit. Invoice creation and PDF render from approved work/lines; immutable versions, authorisation, downloadable private PDF and recorded external payment; no payment gateway.

## Compliance — review gate, not legal advice
Treat jurisdiction, tenure, registered-provider status, potential hazard and formal findings as independent facts; applicability and clocks are not inferred from colour or self-report. Persist awareness, reasonable-belief, investigation, notice, safety and completion events with provenance and corrections. Version rule sets and sources/effective dates, preserve decisions and historical results, working-day exclusions and UK local-time/DST handling, exception/rehousing paths and auditable overrides that cannot silently erase obligations. Unknown or contested inputs must flag human assessment; no definitive automated classification. Expert housing-law review and approval of rules, exceptions, definitions, clock starts and acceptance fixtures required before any production use.

Official research checked 4 November 2026: [2025 regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1042/made); [Phase 1 guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/awaabs-law-guidance-for-social-landlords/awaabs-law-guidance-for-social-landlords-timeframes-for-repairs-in-the-social-rented-sector); [Phase 2 guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/awaabs-law-phase-2-guidance-for-social-housing-landlords/awaabs-law-phase-2-guidance-for-social-landlords); [government collection](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/awaabs-law-in-the-social-rented-sector). Phase 1 commenced 27 October 2025 for significant damp/mould and emergency hazards in qualifying English social housing. Phase 2 guidance describes additional significant hazards from 30 November 2026; confirm enactment and full scope before activating. Guidance describes standard investigation within 10 working days, written summary generally within 3 working days after investigation, and relevant safety work within 5 working days following a significant hazard finding. Emergency timing and triggers require careful treatment of awareness, reasonable belief and investigation, including 24-hour requirements and exceptions. Further/renewed investigations, preventive works, rehousing and exceptional circumstances require explicit review. Do not apply these rules automatically to private landlords; other obligations may apply. No rules engine is approved by this document.

## Migration and rollback
1. Snapshot code, deployment identity, schema and synthetic data inventory; verify tested backup/restore before touching existing D1. No synthetic keys or exports in git. Confirm bridge permissions, source SHA and current deployment with read-only checks.
2. Develop against local isolated database; introduce additive schema and reversible feature flags with migration ledger/checksums. Test upgrades on copied fictional fixtures, old/new compatibility, foreign keys, idempotence, partial failures and rollback; deploy migrations only via a reviewed, explicitly pinned bridge change. Existing `0001.sql` deployment alone is insufficient.
3. Stand up isolated staging if confirmed possible, with independent bindings, secrets and no production data; do not point the existing script at new resources without reviewing its hard-coded names. Never write to AISO/Niches/Xplorer. Exercise full test accounts and failed delivery using fakes, then real test delivery where authorised.
4. Pilot replacement only after restoring backup and rollback rehearsal, feature and security gates, source/D1/build checks and browser verification. Versioned cutover plan includes old Worker source SHA, database snapshot, forward-compatible migration or restoration procedure, freeze/window and owner sign-off. Never delete original data as part of routine deployment. Real data only after Phase 7 gates.

## Phases and measurable acceptance gates
**1. Foundation.** This roadmap, reuse inventory, legal source review, migration plan and independently tested pure priority domain increment. Gate: tests preserve safety flags and immutable history; residents cannot assess; no pilot schema or deployment changes.

**2. Accounts and properties.** Verified registration/recovery, landlord organisation, owner/manager roles and MFA, properties, resident invitations and simple dashboards. Gate: two organisations and five fictional users, invitation replay/expiry and cross-tenant/property denial tested; managers cannot self-elevate; rate limiting and mobile keyboard checks. Genuine data remains blocked pending Phase 7.

**3. Contractors and jobs.** Per-landlord trade directory, identity-bound case invitation, assignment, acceptance, appointments, reassignment, arrival, make-safe, completion and resident dispute. Gate: plumber and electrician receive only their assigned cases; revocation and reassignment retain evidence, stale links fail; missed appointments and concurrent updates tested.

**4. Communication.** Shared and per-contractor private threads with scoped files. Gate: resident cannot invite or read private quotes, contractors cannot read another contractor's private thread or any unrelated case; removal revokes access, history preserved, malicious files and large uploads rejected. Email/push feature only advertised after delivery proven.

**5. Reminders and compliance.** Persistent outbox, retry and escalation, user preferences, backup contacts, audit, separate legally reviewed versioned rules and deadlines. Gate: simulated clock covers unacknowledged emergency, no assignment/response, missed arrival, no make-safe, overdue, dispute, failed delivery and retries; no delivery claimed without evidence; weekends, bank holidays, DST, effective dates, changes in belief, overrides and legal exceptions reviewed with specialist and tested. Engine blocked for real decisions until approved.

**6. Commercial.** Versioned quotes, approvals/rejections, invoices, external payment records and accessible PDFs. Gate: contractor submits/revises, authorised landlord approves, invoice sums/rounding and payment history agree, PDF authorisation and cross-tenant denial tested; no gateway.

**7. Hardening.** Secure file pipeline, independent security testing, privacy/retention/rights and support processes, audited backup/restore, abuse and recovery tests, WCAG 2.2 AA review, physical mobile/device testing, observability and incident ownership. Gate: zero unresolved critical/high security issues, evidenced backup restoration, two independent tenants, law and privacy sign-off, cost/quota review and full end-to-end fictional workflow through browser, API and D1. Explicit operator approval before real personal data.
