# Housing requirements review — 3 November 2026

Scope: initial reference material is for England's social rented sector. Do not apply it automatically to private tenancies or other UK nations. No automatic hazard determination or statutory-deadline calculator is implemented.

Official Phase 1 guidance describes emergency investigation and safety work within 24 hours, significant hazard investigation within 10 working days, a written summary within 3 working days after investigation, and relevant safety work within 5 working days following a significant-hazard investigation. Supplementary preventative work has additional commencement requirements. Awareness, assessment, access, circumstances and the full regulations matter; these summaries cannot determine compliance.

Phase 2 guidance describes additional significant hazard categories taking effect on 30 November 2026. This is a future effective date at this review. Version policies by effective date; do not silently back-apply new rules.

Damp/mould guidance requires investigation of underlying causes; calculations and photographs do not justify blaming residents. HHSRS and fitness/disrepair obligations operate alongside Awaab's Law. A competent human must assess urgency and risk. Do not await sensor readings before responding to a reported emergency.
